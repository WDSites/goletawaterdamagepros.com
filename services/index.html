How they fit together

How the six services connect on a real job

A water loss rarely stays in one category. The first services are time sensitive, and the later ones depend on the earlier ones being finished properly. Seeing the order helps you judge any plan you are given.

  1. Emergency water removal takes out standing water and stops the spread.
  2. Water mitigation dries the structure and records moisture readings.
  3. Water damage cleanup removes materials that cannot dry and cleans what remains.
  4. Flood damage restoration adds sanitizing when the water came from outside or from a drain.
  5. Water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and paint once readings are dry.
  6. Mold remediation is added when growth has started or moisture was left in place.

The EPA advises drying wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth, which is why extraction and drying come before everything else. A company that wants to start with paint or flooring before it has dry readings is working in the wrong order.

How pricing is set

We explain how pricing is set before work starts, and we do not quote a number over the phone because the number depends on what is wet. Cost follows the area affected, the materials involved, the category of water, the equipment days needed and the amount of rebuilding. A written scope lets you compare plans on the same basis.
