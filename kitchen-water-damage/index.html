Kitchen Water Damage in Goleta, CA

Water hides under cabinets and floors. We find it, dry it and prepare the room for repair.

The kitchen is the wettest room in most homes, with a dishwasher, a sink, a refrigerator supply line and often an ice maker, all working within a few feet of one another. A small failure can soak cabinet bases, flooring and the wall behind. If you need kitchen water damage restoration in Goleta, CA, call our 24/7 line and follow the safety steps below.

This page is about the room itself and the way water moves through cabinets, floors and walls. When the source is an appliance, the appliance leak page covers the cause, and this page covers the materials that kitchens are made of.

Why kitchens are different

Kitchen water gets trapped. Toe kicks, cabinet boxes and sink bases are made of particleboard or plywood that swells when wet and does not return to shape. Water also runs under cabinets and behind the dishwasher, where it cannot be seen. Tile or vinyl on top can look fine while the subfloor beneath is soaked.

Goleta kitchens range from older tract homes to newer condominiums and apartments. In a multi-unit building, a kitchen leak often reaches the unit below, so the speed of the response matters beyond your own walls.

First-hour safety steps in a wet kitchen

  1. Close the angle stop under the sink or the main water valve.
  2. Turn off power to the kitchen circuits at the breaker if you can reach it on dry floor.
  3. Unplug the refrigerator, dishwasher and small appliances only if the outlet is dry.
  4. Take food, dishes and pantry items out of wet cabinets.
  5. Mop up surface water and open the cabinet doors to let air in.
  6. Photograph the room and call the 24/7 line.

Water, outlets and gas

Kitchens have outlets at counter height and sometimes a gas line to the range. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility from outside. Do not use a switch or an outlet near water.

How we restore a water damaged kitchen

We extract standing water, then use a moisture meter and a thermal camera to follow it under and behind the cabinets. Toe kicks and damaged cabinet bases are opened or removed so air can reach the wet framing. Small injection drying systems can reach cavities behind tile or hard surfaces. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until the readings match a dry reference.

We reference ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration. Floors are handled on the water damage repair page after drying, and any wet drywall follows the steps on the water mitigation page.

Kitchen water damage and insurance

A sudden leak such as a failed supply line is often covered, while a drip that has run for months is often excluded as long-term seepage. Keep the failed part if you can, photograph the damage before anything is moved and save the repair invoice. Ask your insurer what applies before cabinets are discarded.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not put new flooring over a damp subfloor.
  • Do not leave wet items stored in the cabinet.
  • Do not run a leaking dishwasher to see if it stops.
  • Replace rubber supply hoses with braided lines.
  • Check under the sink every few months.
  • Know where the angle stops and the main shutoff are.

Kitchen water damage questions

Is emergency kitchen water damage restoration available in Goleta?

Yes. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and same-day response is offered when available. Close the shutoff and call.

Can wet cabinets be saved?

Solid wood can often be dried. Particleboard bases that have swollen usually cannot. Moisture readings decide.

Will the floor need to be replaced?

Not always. Hard surfaces over a dry subfloor may stay. A wet subfloor is dried first.

How long does drying take?

Drying commonly takes several days, and the log of readings shows when it is done.
