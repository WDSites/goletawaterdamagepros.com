A sewage backup is a contamination event, not an ordinary leak. Wastewater coming up through a floor drain, toilet or tub carries bacteria and must be handled with containment and protective gear. If you need emergency sewage backup cleanup in Goleta, CA, call our 24/7 line, keep everyone out of the area and follow the safety steps below.

We contain the area, remove the sewage and affected materials, clean and sanitize surfaces and dry the structure. A plumber or the sanitary district clears the blockage. The cleanup and the clearing are separate jobs, and the cause decides who does what.

Where a sewage backup starts

Backups start either in your private lateral, the line that runs from the house to the public main, or in the public main itself. Grease, roots, a collapsed section and a clog in the lateral are common private causes. A blockage in the main can push wastewater up through the lowest drain in the house.

The Goleta Sanitary District says the property owner is responsible for maintaining the sewer lateral, and that the district handles problems in its main. If the cause is in the private line, the district advises calling a licensed plumber. That is why the first question after a backup is where the blockage is.

First-hour safety steps after a sewage backup

  1. Stop using water: no flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher.
  2. Keep children, pets and everyone else out of the affected area.
  3. Turn off power to the area at the breaker only if you can reach it on dry floor.
  4. Do not touch the water with bare hands, and do not use household vacuums.
  5. Wash hands thoroughly after any contact.
  6. Call the 24/7 line, and call a plumber or the sanitary district to find the blockage.

Sewage is contaminated water

Raw sewage is treated as Category 3 water under industry practice. Do not clean it with a mop and a bucket, and do not run fans that blow it around.

How we clean up a sewage backup

The crew sets up containment so contaminated air and water do not spread. Standing sewage is extracted, and porous materials such as carpet, pad, insulation and the lower part of drywall are removed and bagged. Hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized, and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers until readings match a dry reference.

We reference ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration, which sorts sewage into the most contaminated category. The wider flood process is on the flood damage restoration page, and removal steps are on the water damage cleanup page.

Sewage backups and insurance

Many homeowners policies treat a sewer or drain backup differently from other water damage, and coverage often depends on an added endorsement. The California Department of Insurance notes that standard homeowners policies exclude flood. Photograph the damage, keep every invoice and ask your insurer what applies before work is signed off.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not keep using water after a backup starts.
  • Do not try to clean raw sewage yourself with household tools.
  • Do not pour grease down the drain.
  • Have the lateral inspected with a camera if backups have happened before.
  • Ask a plumber about a backwater valve.
  • Know where your cleanout is.

Local note: sewer backups in Goleta

The Goleta Sanitary District states that the property owner maintains the sewer lateral. It says it responds around the clock for main line problems, and that a district permit is needed to replace a lateral or repair one outside the property line or in a district easement.

The district also says installing a lateral liner is not a district-approved repair, and that an owner can be liable for damage if a liner protrudes into the main.

The Census Bureau lists Goleta as 50.7 percent owner-occupied. If you rent, report a backup to the landlord in writing the same day.

Who clears the blockage?

A plumber if it is in your private lateral, and the sanitary district if it is in the public main. We clean up the damage.

Can sewage cleanup wait until morning?

It is better not to wait. Contaminated water soaks into porous materials and spreads quickly.

Which materials are thrown away?

Porous materials the sewage touched, such as carpet, pad and wet drywall, are normally removed. Hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized.
