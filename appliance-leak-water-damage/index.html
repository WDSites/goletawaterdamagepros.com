Water from a washer, dishwasher or refrigerator travels farther than it looks. We dry it all.

Appliance leaks are among the most common sources of household water damage, and they often run for a long time before anyone notices. A washing machine hose, a dishwasher line, a refrigerator ice maker connection or a sink drain can leak behind a unit or under a floor. If you need appliance leak water damage cleanup in Goleta, CA, call our 24/7 line and follow the safety steps below.

We remove the water, dry the floor, wall and cabinet area and prepare the room for repair. A technician or plumber repairs the appliance or its connection. Fixing the source comes first, since drying a room that keeps getting wet does not work.

Where appliance leaks come from

Washing machines fail at the inlet hoses and the drain connection. Dishwashers leak at the door seal, the supply line and the drain hose. Refrigerators leak at the ice maker line and the water filter connection. Water heaters and sinks are separate sources with their own pages, so we cover the appliance side here.

The hidden part is the path. Water from a washer or dishwasher often runs under flooring, into the subfloor and along the bottom of walls. It can wet cabinets, baseboards and the ceiling in the room below, which is why the visible puddle is rarely the whole loss.

First-hour safety steps after an appliance leak

  1. Unplug the appliance or turn off its circuit if you can reach it on dry floor.
  2. Close the supply valves behind or under the appliance.
  3. Mop and soak up surface water, and lift rugs and mats.
  4. Move the appliance only if the floor is dry enough to stand on safely.
  5. Photograph the appliance, the hose or line and the wet area.
  6. Call the 24/7 line and tell us what leaked and how long it may have been going on.

Do not slide a wet appliance across a wet floor

Appliances are heavy, and a wet floor can fail underneath them. Leave heavy units in place until the area has been checked.

How we handle an appliance leak

We find the extent of the water with moisture meters and a thermal camera, then extract what we can reach. Baseboards, toe kicks and damaged flooring come off where water has gone behind them. Drying mats, air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the subfloor, and readings are logged until they match a dry reference.

We reference ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration. Kitchens have a few extra concerns, which the kitchen water damage page covers. Rebuilding after drying is handled through water damage repair.

Appliance leaks and insurance

A sudden leak that floods a floor is often covered as water damage under a homeowners policy, while seepage that went on for weeks or months is often excluded. The appliance itself may fall under a warranty or a home service plan instead. Photograph the unit and its connections, keep receipts and ask your insurer what applies.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not ignore a small puddle that returns.
  • Do not replace the flooring before the subfloor is dry.
  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided lines on a regular schedule.
  • Close the washer valves when you leave for long trips.
  • Check the dishwasher and refrigerator lines yearly.
  • Consider a leak sensor on the floor behind each unit.

Local note: appliance leaks in Goleta homes

The Census Bureau lists Goleta as 50.7 percent owner-occupied for 2020 to 2024, so many appliance leaks happen in rentals. California Civil Code section 1941.1 lists plumbing in good working order among a landlord's duties, so report a leak in writing the same day.

In a condominium or apartment, water from a washer can reach the unit below. Tell the association or landlord at once and keep photographs.

For plumbing changes behind an appliance, the City of Goleta Building and Safety Division at (805) 961-7552 can say whether a permit is needed.

Which appliance leaks the most?

Washing machine hoses and dishwasher lines are common sources, and the leak is often slow and hidden.

Should I replace the flooring right away?

No. Wait for dry moisture readings. Closing a damp floor is how warping and mold start.

Does a leak under an appliance affect the room below?

It can. Water travels through the subfloor and appears as a ceiling stain in the room underneath.
