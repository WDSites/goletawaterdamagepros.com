About us

Who we are and how we work

Goleta Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration and mold remediation service at 75 Aero Camino in Goleta, California. We answer one 24/7 emergency line, and we handle water removal, drying, cleanup, repair, flood work and mold remediation for homes and small businesses in Goleta and nearby Santa Barbara County towns.

There are no staff names, biographies or stock stories on this site. The useful information is how a loss is handled, what each stage involves and what you can expect to be asked, so that is what these pages describe. Every service page explains its own process, and the blog goes deeper on the questions callers ask most often.

What we do

  • Remove standing water after burst pipes, overflows and appliance failures.
  • Dry structures with air movers and dehumidifiers, and record moisture readings.
  • Clean up and remove materials that cannot be dried in place.
  • Rebuild drywall, ceilings, flooring and paint once readings are dry.
  • Handle storm runoff, groundwater and sewage-related water.
  • Remediate mold with containment, removal and cleaning when moisture has fed growth.
Process

How a job runs from the first call

The first call is about safety and the water source. We ask where the water started, which rooms are wet, how long it has been there and whether it has been stopped. We tell you what to shut off and what to leave alone, because the first hour often decides how much has to be replaced.

We then arrange an assessment with moisture meters and thermal imaging, explain the scope of work and how pricing is set, and begin extraction and drying. Readings are taken at the start and rechecked as the equipment runs, so you can see the numbers move toward normal.

Repairs begin only when readings are dry. Rebuilding over damp framing is how stains and mold return, so the repair stage waits for the drying stage to be finished and documented.

Standards

Standards we point to

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published industry standard for water damage restoration, and S520 is the published standard for mold remediation. We name them so you can ask any company, including us, whether its crews work to them.

We also point to public guidance. The EPA advises drying wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth, and the California Department of Insurance explains that standard homeowners insurance does not cover flood damage. Those two facts shape how fast we ask you to act and why we ask you to read your policy early.

What we do not claim

We do not promise an arrival time, a claim outcome or a particular drying time. We do not offer waterproofing, drainage work, roofing or exterior siding repair. If a job needs a different trade, we say so.

Local context

Why the local details matter

Goleta sits on the coast of Santa Barbara County, and the Census Bureau puts the city at 50.7 percent owner-occupied. That means a large share of the calls involve a landlord, a property manager or a condominium association, and the first steps often include written notice and photographs before anything is removed.

The same source lists the median value of an owner-occupied home at $1,062,100 for 2020 to 2024. With values like that, a repair that matches the existing finishes and leaves a clear record of readings and photographs matters when a home is sold or refinanced.

For permits, the City of Goleta Building and Safety Division can say whether a repair needs one. Rules vary by project, so we suggest calling the division at (805) 961-7552 before structural, plumbing or electrical work begins.

How we handle sensitive jobs

Sewage, flood water and mold call for extra care. Contaminated porous materials are removed and bagged before they are carried through the home, work areas are separated from clean areas and surfaces are cleaned and dried before anything is rebuilt. We explain each step as it happens and ask before anything is discarded.
