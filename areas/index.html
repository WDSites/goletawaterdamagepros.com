Before you call

What helps us confirm coverage quickly

Have the street address and the nearest cross street ready, along with the floor or room that is wet. If the building is a condominium or an apartment, tell us the unit and whether neighbors are affected, because water that crosses a wall or ceiling brings an association or a landlord into the job.

Tell us where the water started and whether it has stopped. A supply line that is still running is a different first step from a ceiling that stopped dripping an hour ago. We explain what to shut off, what to photograph and what to leave in place while you wait.

  • The address, ZIP code and nearest cross street.
  • The room or floor affected and the source if you know it.
  • Whether the water is clean, from a drain or from outside.
  • Whether you own the property, rent it or manage it for someone else.
  • Photographs of the damage, taken from a safe spot.
