Roof leak water damage starts above you and ends in a ceiling, a wall or an attic floor. By the time a stain shows on the ceiling, water has usually been moving through insulation and framing for a while. We handle the wet interior: extraction, drying, removal of soaked materials and preparation for repair. We do not repair roofs. A roofer fixes the opening, and that repair should happen quickly, because the same hole will let in the next storm.

How roof leaks reach the inside of a Goleta home

Water enters at worn flashing, cracked sealant, lifted shingles or tiles, clogged gutters and around skylights and vents. It rarely drops straight down. It runs along rafters and pipes and shows up several feet away from the hole, which is why the stain and the leak are often in different places.

First-hour safety steps for a roof leak

  1. Keep people out from under a bulging or sagging ceiling.
  2. Turn off power to the affected room at the breaker if you can reach it on dry floor.
  3. Move furniture and place buckets, and leave the ceiling alone until it has been checked.
  4. Do not climb on a wet roof. Ask a roofer to tarp it.
  5. Photograph the ceiling, the attic and the outside of the roof if you can see it safely.
  6. Call the 24/7 line and tell us how large the stain is and whether anything is dripping.

A sagging ceiling can fall

Wet drywall is heavy and weak. If the ceiling bulges, stay out of the room and call. A trained crew opens it in a controlled way and drains the water.

How we handle roof leak water damage

We start with moisture meters and a thermal camera to find the wet area behind the ceiling. Soaked insulation and drywall are removed back to dry material, and any trapped water is released safely. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity, and readings are logged until they match a dry reference.

We follow ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration. When the structure is dry, the room is ready for water damage repair, and if growth has started the next step is mold remediation.

Roof leaks and insurance

Insurers often treat a sudden storm opening differently from long-term wear. A roof that failed because of age or lack of maintenance is often excluded, while damage from a covered storm event may be covered. Photograph the roof, the ceiling and the date, keep receipts for temporary repairs and ask your insurer before permanent work.

Mistakes to avoid and how to prevent roof leaks

  • Do not paint over a stain before the roof is fixed.
  • Do not walk on a wet roof.
  • Do not ignore a small stain that returns after rain.
  • Clear gutters and downspouts before the wet season.
  • Have flashing and sealant checked after a heavy storm.
  • Look in the attic after the first big rain each year.

Do you repair the roof?

No. A roofer repairs the opening. We handle the wet insulation, drywall and framing inside.

Why is the stain in a different place from the hole?

Water travels along rafters, pipes and the top of the ceiling before it shows. The stain is where it finally reaches the board.

Can I wait until the rain stops to call?

It is better not to. Wet insulation and drywall stay wet, and the EPA advises drying within 24 to 48 hours.
