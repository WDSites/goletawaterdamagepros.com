AC Leak Water Damage Repair in Goleta, CA

Condensate leaks hide in ceilings and attics. We find the wet area, dry it and prepare repair.

Call (805) 291-8940

An air conditioner leak is usually a slow problem. The system pulls moisture out of the air, and that condensate has to drain away through a pan and a line. When the line clogs, the pan cracks or the coil freezes and melts, water ends up in a ceiling, an attic or a wall. If you need AC leak water damage repair in Goleta, CA, call our 24/7 line and follow the safety steps below.

We handle the wet building materials: extraction, drying, removal of soaked insulation and drywall, and preparation for repair. An HVAC technician fixes the unit. Both steps matter, because the leak will return if the cause is not corrected.

How common is this in Goleta

Cooling is not as universal on this stretch of the coast as it is inland, and plenty of homes rely on mild weather instead. Where central air, ductless mini splits or heat pumps are installed, condensate leaks are a real source of water damage, and we cover the real cases here without overstating how many there are.

The usual locations are an attic air handler over a ceiling, a closet unit in a hallway, a wall mounted ductless head and an outdoor line that drains near a foundation. A newer mini split can drip onto a wall for weeks and leave a stain that looks like a roof leak.

First-hour safety steps for an AC leak

  1. Turn off the air conditioner at the thermostat and at the breaker.
  2. Stay out from under a ceiling that is stained, bulging or dripping.
  3. Soak up surface water and place a container under any drip.
  4. Do not run the system until a technician has cleared the drain.
  5. Photograph the unit, the drip and the damaged area.
  6. Call the 24/7 line and tell us where the unit is and how large the stain is.

Water near electrical equipment

Air handlers and ductless heads carry power and control wiring. Do not touch a wet unit, and keep the circuit off until a technician has checked it.

How we handle AC leak water damage

We use moisture meters and a thermal camera to find the wet area in the ceiling or wall, because condensate often travels along framing before it shows. Soaked insulation and drywall are removed back to dry material. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity, and readings are logged until they match a dry reference.

We reference ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration. Clean condensate becomes a mold concern if it sits, so the EPA advice to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours applies. Related steps are on the water mitigation page and the water damage repair page.

AC leaks and insurance

Sudden and accidental water damage from a system failure is often covered, while a drain that has leaked slowly for a long time often is not. Maintenance records can help show that the unit was kept up. Photograph the unit and the stain, keep the HVAC invoice and ask your insurer what applies.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not keep running a unit that is dripping.
  • Do not paint over the stain before the line has been cleared.
  • Have the condensate line flushed once a year.
  • Add a float switch that shuts the system off when the pan fills.
  • Replace filters on schedule so coils do not freeze.
  • Check the ceiling below an attic unit each season.
